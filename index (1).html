Quick Answers

Will it tighten a sagging neck?

Don’t expect a surgical-style lift. Some reviewers noticed a smoother look on the neck or under the eyes after a few weeks; others used it for a month and saw no change in wrinkles. Think of it as a moisturizer that may soften the look of lines.

Is it really fragrance-free?

The Amazon listing marks it unscented and the published ingredient list has no added perfume. Many reviewers liked that. A few still noticed a faint smell, which can come from raw ingredients.

Can sensitive skin use it?

The brand says it passed a skin irritation test, but reviews are mixed: most report no problems, while some reported rashes, itching, swelling, or breakouts. Patch-test first and stop at the first sign of a reaction.

What about cheaper lookalike jars?

This review covers only the cream on the official Amazon listing (ASIN B0FQBL8S3K). Lookalike jars that copy the name, color, or claims may not be the same formula, so check the brand and seller before you buy.
