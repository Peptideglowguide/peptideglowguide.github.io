What’s in it, and what that means

This is a 50 ml jar of EQQUALBERRY NAD+ Peptide Boosting Firming Cream (Amazon ASIN B0FQBL8S3K). The brand advertises liposomal NAD+ at 5%, a blend of 13 peptides, and ceramide. The published ingredient list does include nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide (NAD+), Ceramide NP, and a cluster of peptides, and it has no added perfume, which matches the unscented / fragrance-free listing.

Two important caveats. First, NAD+ and the peptides appear toward the end of the ingredient list. Independent ingredient reviewers have questioned whether the headline “5% NAD+” claim matches that placement, and we did not find a finished-product lab report that independently verifies the percentage. Treat 5% NAD+ and the 13-peptide blend as brand claims unless you see verified testing. Second, topical NAD+ is still an emerging cosmetic ingredient; a cream is not a proven medical treatment for sagging skin.

The formula also includes more familiar moisturizing ingredients such as glycerin, niacinamide, panthenol, squalane, and adenosine. Those are a more straightforward reason the cream may feel hydrating, even if the NAD+ headline is what the ads emphasize.

Who it’s for

This cream is a reasonable try if you want a fragrance-free moisturizer and you’re willing to judge it over several weeks, not a few days. It may suit someone looking to soften the look of fine lines and keep the face and neck comfortably hydrated, especially if you already like Korean-style creams and can patch-test first.

It is a weaker fit if you want a dramatic lift, have very reactive skin, or are shopping from an ad that promises a tightened “turkey neck.” A moisturizer can improve how skin looks and feels. FAQ

Will this tighten a sagging neck?

Don’t expect a surgical-style lift. Some reviewers noticed a slightly smoother look on the neck or under-eyes after a few weeks. Others used it for a month and saw no wrinkle change. Think of it as a moisturizer that might soften the look of lines, not a procedure.

Is it really fragrance-free?

The Amazon listing marks it unscented, the published ingredient list has no added perfume, and many reviewers specifically liked that it has no fragrance. A few people still mentioned a smell, which can happen with raw ingredients even when no perfume is added.

Can people with sensitive skin use it?

The brand says the cream passed a skin irritation test. Public reviews are mixed: some people reported no irritation at all, and others reported rashes, itching, swelling, or breakouts. If your skin is reactive, patch-test first and stop at the first sign of a problem.
