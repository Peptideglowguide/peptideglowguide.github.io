Quick Answers

How loud is it?

Loud. Even happy reviewers mention the noise. It’s roughly blender-level, but it only runs for a couple of minutes per spin.

Do I really have to freeze for 24 hours?

Yes. Ninja’s directions say to prep the base and freeze it for 24 hours. A pint that isn’t fully and evenly frozen tends to come out icy or uneven, so many owners keep a few pints in the freezer in rotation.

Can I use any protein shake?

Most ready-to-drink shakes work. Thinner, lower-sugar shakes are the most likely to come out powdery on the first spin, and a splash of milk plus Re-spin usually fixes it.

Which extra pints fit the NC301?

The NC301 comes with two 16 oz pints and lids. If you buy more, pick pints listed as compatible with the NC301 or the original CREAMi. The larger pints made for CREAMi Deluxe models are a different size.

Will it help me lose weight?

No appliance can promise that. The CREAMi just freezes and spins whatever you put in it, so the calories and protein come from your ingredients. Talk with your clinician or dietitian about your own goals.
